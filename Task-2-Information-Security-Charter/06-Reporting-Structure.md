## 6. Reporting Structure

- IT and business teams report security issues, risks and incidents to the Information Security Governance function.
- The Director of Information Security Governance provides regular security and risk reports to the CEO and Security Governance/Steering Committee.
- Executive Management reviews significant risks, incidents, compliance issues and resource requirements.
- The Board receives regular high-level security and risk reports and provides oversight.
- Critical security incidents, major risks or issues affecting patient information, operations, reputation or regulatory compliance must be escalated immediately.
