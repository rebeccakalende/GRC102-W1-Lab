## 5. Key Principles

- **Risk-based:** Security decisions will be based on the likelihood and potential impact of identified risks.
- **Business-aligned:** Security will support GHC's business goals, including growth, innovation, customer trust, and operational efficiency.
- **Proportionate:** Security controls will match the level of risk and should not create unnecessary barriers to business operations.
- **Continuously improving:** GHC will regularly review its security practices, learn from incidents, and improve controls as risks and technology change.
