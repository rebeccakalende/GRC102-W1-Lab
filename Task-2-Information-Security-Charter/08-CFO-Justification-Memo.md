# CFO Justification Memo

**To:** Marcus Thorne, CFO  
**From:** Director, Information Security Governance  
**Subject:** Justification for the GHC Information Security Charter

The Information Security Charter provides GHC with a clear governance structure for protecting patient information, managing security risks and meeting applicable healthcare and data-protection obligations. It also supports our strategic goals of growth, operational efficiency, customer trust, innovation and regulatory excellence.

The Charter improves accountability by clearly defining responsibilities and reporting relationships. It also provides a basis for prioritising security investments according to business risk and impact. This will help GHC make informed investment decisions while supporting continued growth, acquisitions and development of AI-enabled and digital-health capabilities.
