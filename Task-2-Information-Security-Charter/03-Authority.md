## 3. Authority

The GHC Information Security Programme operates under the authority of the Board and Chief Executive Officer (CEO).

The Director of Information Security Governance is responsible for coordinating the programme and has authority to establish security requirements, monitor compliance, and escalate significant risks to executive management and the Board.

Business and technology leaders are responsible for implementing approved security requirements within their areas. Significant security risks, incidents, policy exceptions, or matters that may affect GHC's business objectives must be escalated through the established governance structure.
