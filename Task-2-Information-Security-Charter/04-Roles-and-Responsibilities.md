## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Board of Directors:** Provides overall oversight of information security and holds executive management accountable.
- **CEO:** Provides executive direction and ensures security supports GHC's strategic goals.
- **Director, Information Security Governance:** Leads security governance, risk management, policies, reporting and escalation.
- **IT Manager:** Implements and maintains technical security controls and supports incident response.
- **Compliance Officer:** Oversees applicable healthcare regulatory and compliance requirements.
- **Senior Developer:** Ensures security requirements are considered during product development.
- **Finance:** Supports protection of financial information and security investment decisions.
- **HR Manager:** Incorporates security requirements into onboarding and offboarding.
