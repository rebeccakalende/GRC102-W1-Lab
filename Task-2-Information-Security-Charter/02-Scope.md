## 2. Scope

GHC's Information Security Programme applies to all organisational assets, systems, information, employees, contractors, and third parties that support or have access to GHC's operations.

This includes:

- Patient, customer, financial, employee and business information.
- Cloud platforms, applications, networks and databases.
- Employees, contractors and other authorised users.
- Suppliers, partners and service providers.
- Physical equipment and supporting facilities.
- Systems and processes introduced through acquisitions and future business growth.
