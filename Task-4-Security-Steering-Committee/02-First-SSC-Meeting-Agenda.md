# GHC Security Steering Committee
## First Meeting Agenda

**Date:** [10/09/2026]  
**Time:** [08:00]  
**Chair:** CEO / Delegated Executive  
**Meeting:** First Security Steering Committee Meeting

### Agenda

1. **Opening and Introductions**
   - Welcome and introduction of SSC members.
   - Confirm the purpose and responsibilities of the committee.

2. **Review of SSC Terms of Reference**
   - Confirm scope, membership, responsibilities and decision authority.

3. **Current Security Risk Overview**
   - Review key security risks and the recent data-leakage near-miss.
   - Identify issues requiring immediate attention.

4. **Password Policy Conflict**
   - Review the proposed 16-character complex password requirement with changes every 30 days.
   - Consider the CTO's recommendation to prioritise multi-factor authentication and secure password management.
   - Assess security benefits, user impact and business productivity.
   - Agree on a balanced and risk-based approach.

5. **Security Governance Priorities**
   - Review priority governance improvements.
   - Confirm responsibilities and actions.

6. **Decisions and Action Items**
   - Record decisions made.
   - Assign owners and deadlines for agreed actions.

7. **Any Other Business**

8. **Next Meeting**
   - Confirm date and key topics for the next SSC meeting.
