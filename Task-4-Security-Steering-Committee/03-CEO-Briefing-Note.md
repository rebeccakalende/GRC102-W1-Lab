# CEO Briefing Note

**To:** Sarah Chen, CEO  
**From:** Director, Information Security Governance  
**Subject:** Security Steering Committee and Cross-Functional Decision-Making

The Security Steering Committee (SSC) will provide a formal forum for resolving disagreements between technology, security and business teams. Instead of allowing individual departments to make decisions independently, the SSC will consider the security risk, business impact, regulatory requirements and operational needs before reaching a decision. The password-policy disagreement will be assessed using this balanced approach.

The SSC will also ensure that decisions are documented, responsibilities are assigned and significant matters are escalated to executive management or the Board when necessary. This approach will help GHC maintain strong security governance while supporting business objectives such as innovation, operational efficiency, customer trust and growth.
