# GHC Security Steering Committee
## Terms of Reference

### 1. Purpose

The Security Steering Committee (SSC) provides cross-functional direction and oversight for information security at GHC. It supports informed security decisions, resolves conflicts and ensures security remains aligned with business objectives.

### 2. Scope

The SSC covers information security policies, risks, incidents, compliance, security investments, technology decisions and other matters that require cross-functional security decisions.

### 3. Membership

The SSC will include:

- CEO or delegated executive – Chair
- Director, Information Security Governance
- CTO
- IT Manager
- Compliance Officer
- Finance representative
- HR representative
- Relevant business or development representative when required

### 4. Responsibilities

The SSC will:

- Review and approve security priorities and recommendations.
- Review significant security risks and incidents.
- Resolve cross-functional security disagreements.
- Review security policies and proposed changes.
- Monitor security performance and compliance.
- Support appropriate security investment decisions.
- Ensure security decisions remain aligned with GHC's business objectives.

### 5. Meeting Cadence

The SSC will meet monthly, with additional meetings called when a significant security issue requires urgent attention.

### 6. Decision Authority

The SSC may approve security decisions within its delegated authority. Matters involving significant business risk, major investment, regulatory impact or strategic decisions will be escalated to the CEO or Board as appropriate.

### 7. Reporting

The Director of Information Security Governance will maintain records of SSC decisions and provide relevant security and risk updates to executive management and the Board.

### 8. Review

These Terms of Reference will be reviewed annually or when there are significant changes to GHC's business, technology or security risk environment.
