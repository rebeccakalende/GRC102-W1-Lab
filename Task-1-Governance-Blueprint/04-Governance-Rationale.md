# GHC Governance Rationale

The proposed governance structure gives GHC clear accountability by assigning specific responsibilities to the Board, CEO, Information Security Governance function, IT, Compliance and other business departments. The RACI matrix further clarifies who is responsible for activities and who is accountable for decisions.

It improves transparency because security risks, incidents, policies and performance can be reported through defined channels to management and the Board.

The structure also supports business alignment by involving technology, compliance, finance, HR and other business functions in security decisions. This is important because GHC needs to protect patient information while continuing to grow and develop AI-enabled and digital-health capabilities.

Finally, it strengthens risk management by creating a formal process for identifying, reviewing, prioritising and escalating security risks instead of relying on a reactive approach.
