# Board Executive Summary – Governance Maturity

## Current Position

GHC's current information security governance maturity is generally between Level 1 – Ad Hoc and Level 2 – Initial. The main weaknesses are unclear security ownership, reactive risk management, inconsistent policies, limited translation of technical metrics into business impact, low training engagement, and an audit-driven approach to compliance. These gaps create increased exposure to security, operational, regulatory and reputational risks.

## Recommended Direction

Over the next 12–18 months, GHC should focus on moving all six governance domains to at least Level 3 – Defined. The priority should be to formalise governance and accountability, standardise policies, establish proactive risk management, improve Board-level reporting and security awareness, and strengthen continuous compliance monitoring. Progress should be measured through clear targets and reviewed regularly by management and the Security Steering Committee.

Achieving Level 3 will give GHC a more consistent and accountable governance environment that supports the organisation's strategic goals of growth, operational efficiency, customer trust, innovation and regulatory excellence.
