# GHC Governance Maturity Assessment

| Governance Domain | Current Level | Reason |
|---|---:|---|
| Policy & Documentation | 2 – Initial | Policies exist, but they are inconsistent, inherited from acquisitions, and not always current. |
| Roles & Responsibilities | 1 – Ad Hoc | Security ownership and accountability are unclear. |
| Risk Management | 1 – Ad Hoc | The organisation is reactive and has no formal proactive assessment process. |
| Metrics & Reporting | 2 – Initial | Technical metrics exist, but they are not consistently translated into business impact. |
| Training & Awareness | 2 – Initial | Annual training is mandatory, but employee engagement is low. |
| Compliance | 2 – Initial | Compliance preparation is mainly audit-driven rather than continuously monitored. |

## Overall Assessment

GHC's current governance maturity is low, with most areas at Level 1 or 2. The biggest weaknesses are unclear accountability and reactive risk management. GHC therefore needs to formalise its governance processes before it can consistently operate at Level 3.
