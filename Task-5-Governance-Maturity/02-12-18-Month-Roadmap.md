# GHC 12–18 Month Governance Maturity Roadmap

## Roadmap Objective

The objective is to move GHC from its current Level 1–2 governance maturity to at least Level 3 – Defined across all six governance domains within 12–18 months. The roadmap focuses on establishing documented processes, clear accountability, proactive risk management, meaningful reporting, improved awareness, and continuous compliance monitoring.

| Priority Initiative | Timeline | Objective | Key Activities | Measurable Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Formalise Governance and Accountability | Months 1–3 | Establish clear security ownership and decision-making authority. | Approve the Information Security Charter; implement the RACI matrix; establish the Security Steering Committee; define reporting relationships. | 100% of key security activities have an assigned accountable owner by Month 3. |
| 2. Standardise Policies and Documentation | Months 1–4 | Create a consistent and current security policy framework. | Review existing and inherited policies; identify gaps; update policies; obtain approval; communicate policies to staff. | 100% of critical security policies are reviewed, approved and current by Month 4. |
| 3. Establish Formal Risk Management | Months 3–6 | Move GHC from reactive to proactive security risk management. | Create an enterprise security risk register; conduct regular risk assessments; assign risk owners; define treatment plans; review risks through the SSC. | 100% of high risks have an assigned owner and documented treatment plan by Month 6. |
| 4. Improve Metrics, Reporting and Awareness | Months 4–9 | Translate security performance into business-focused information for management and the Board. | Establish Board-level security metrics; introduce regular reporting; improve security awareness activities; use targeted phishing simulations and training. | Quarterly Board security reporting is established, with agreed metrics linked to business risk; training completion reaches at least 90%. |
| 5. Strengthen Continuous Compliance and Review | Months 6–18 | Move compliance from an audit-driven approach to continuous monitoring and improvement. | Map key regulatory obligations; establish compliance monitoring; conduct periodic reviews; track findings and corrective actions; perform annual governance maturity reviews. | Key regulatory obligations are mapped and reviewed regularly, with corrective actions tracked to completion. |

## Implementation Phases

### Phase 1 – Foundation: Months 1–3
- Establish governance structure and accountability.
- Approve the Information Security Charter.
- Implement the RACI matrix.
- Establish the Security Steering Committee.
- Begin policy and documentation review.

### Phase 2 – Standardisation: Months 3–6
- Complete critical policy updates.
- Establish the security risk register.
- Assign risk owners and treatment plans.
- Begin regular security risk reviews.

### Phase 3 – Measurement and Improvement: Months 6–12
- Introduce regular Board-level security reporting.
- Strengthen security awareness and training.
- Establish continuous compliance monitoring.
- Track governance performance against defined targets.

### Phase 4 – Review and Optimisation: Months 12–18
- Conduct a governance maturity reassessment.
- Review the effectiveness of governance processes.
- Address remaining maturity gaps.
- Update the governance roadmap based on results and lessons learned.

## Expected Result

By the end of the 12–18 month period, GHC should have documented and standardised governance processes, clear security accountability, proactive risk management, meaningful Board reporting, stronger employee awareness, and more continuous compliance monitoring. These improvements should enable GHC to operate at least at Level 3 – Defined across all six governance domains.
