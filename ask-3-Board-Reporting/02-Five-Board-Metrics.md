# Five Board-Level Security Metrics

| Metric | September | February | Trend | Board-Level Meaning |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Phishing incidents | 150 | 320 | Increasing | Shows a growing risk of account compromise and possible data exposure. |
| Malware incidents | 25 | 50 | Increasing | Indicates increasing exposure to malicious activity. |
| Critical vulnerabilities patched | 10/15 | 22/40 | Increasing number patched, but coverage remains weak | Important security weaknesses are not being remediated quickly enough. |
| Patching percentage | 67% | 55% | Decreasing | Shows that vulnerability remediation performance is getting worse. |
| High-risk incidents | 2 | 7 | Increasing | Indicates that the organisation's exposure to serious security incidents is increasing. |

## Board Commentary

The overall trend is concerning. Phishing and malware incidents have increased, while patching performance has declined and high-risk incidents have increased significantly. Although more critical vulnerabilities were patched in February than in September, the total number of critical vulnerabilities also increased, meaning GHC is not keeping pace with its security exposure.

The increase in security awareness training from 45% to 70% is positive, but the continued rise in phishing incidents shows that further action is needed.
