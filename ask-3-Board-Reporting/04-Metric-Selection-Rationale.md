# Metric Selection Rationale

The five selected metrics are appropriate for Board oversight because they provide a balanced view of GHC's security posture. Phishing and malware show the level of security threat activity, while critical vulnerabilities patched and overall patching percentage show how effectively GHC is addressing known weaknesses. High-risk incidents show the potential business impact of security events.

Together, these metrics help the Board understand where security risks are increasing, whether controls are working, and where management needs to take action. They are useful for Board-level decision-making because they can be linked to business risk, operational impact and resource requirements.
