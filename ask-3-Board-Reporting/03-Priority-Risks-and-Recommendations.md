# Priority Risks and Recommendations

## Priority Risk 1: Increasing Phishing and High-Risk Incidents

**Risk:** Phishing incidents increased from 150 to 320, while high-risk incidents increased from 2 to 7.

**Recommendation:** Strengthen phishing controls through targeted awareness training, simulated phishing exercises, multi-factor authentication, and closer monitoring of high-risk incidents.

## Priority Risk 2: Declining Vulnerability Patching Performance

**Risk:** Patching performance declined from 67% to 55%, while critical vulnerabilities increased from 15 to 40.

**Recommendation:** Introduce risk-based patching deadlines, assign clear ownership for critical vulnerabilities, and provide regular management reports on overdue vulnerabilities.
